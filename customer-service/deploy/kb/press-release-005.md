### 中文 (zh)

澳門，2026年6月25日

澳門電力股份有限公司（澳電）與中國建築科學研究院建築防火研究所（防火所）於6月22日在北京正式簽署《戰略合作協議》，雙方合作邁入新階段。根據協議，雙方將在標準編制、重大科研技術聯合攻關、工程咨詢與服務、戰略性新興產業等領域開展合作。簽約儀式由澳電人力資源及可持續發展部總監陸希蕾與防火所副所長劉松濤代表雙方簽署，澳電執行委員會首席顧問岳宗斌與防火所副所長劉文利共同見證。澳電客戶服務部總監周環宙亦出席是次儀式。

岳宗斌表示，電力安全是城市公共安全的重要基石，澳電始終將安全生產置於重要位置。此次合作旨在將澳電的電力運營實戰經驗與防火所的科研標準優勢深度融合，為提升電力系統消防安全水平、保障澳門用電安全和能源可持續發展貢獻力量。

今年是國家「十五五」規劃的開局之年，「能源強國」概念首次納入國家五年規劃，粵港澳大灣區建設亦進入新階段。澳門「三五規劃」同步啟航，作為大灣區核心城市，澳門正積極融入國家發展大局，推動綠色低碳轉型發展。電力系統安全穩定運行事關城市運轉、民生保障和能源戰略全域。隨著新能源大規模接入、儲能設施和充電網絡加速佈局，電力系統消防安全面臨新挑戰。澳電與防火所的合作，不僅為澳電配合特區政府推進都市更新、提升居民樓宇供電安全等工作提供了堅實的技術支撐，更是助力粵港澳大灣區高質量發展的重要舉措。雙方將藉此合作深化電力消防安全領域的協同創新，為澳門能源安全與可持續發展持續注入動力。

Macao, 25 June 2026

Companhia de Electricidade de Macau – CEM, S.A. (CEM) and the Institute of Building Fire Research of China Academy of Building Research (CABR Institute of Building Fire Research) officially signed a “Strategic Cooperation Agreement” in Beijing on 22 June, marking a new chapter in collaboration between the two parties. Under the agreement, both parties will cooperate across areas including standards development, joint research and technological breakthroughs, engineering consultancy and services, and strategic emerging industries. The agreement was signed by Hellen Lok, Director of Human Resources and Sustainability Department of CEM, and Liu Songtao, Deputy Director of CABR Institute of Building Fire Research, on behalf of their respective organisations. The signing was witnessed by Benjamin Yue, Principal Advisor to the Executive Committee of CEM, and Liu Wenli, Deputy Director of CABR Institute of Building Fire Research. Chris Chao, Director of Customer Services Department of CEM, also attended the ceremony.

Benjamin Yue stated that electrical safety is a cornerstone of urban public safety, and that CEM has always regarded safe operations as a matter of paramount importance. This collaboration aims to deeply integrate CEM’s operational expertise in power supply with CABR Institute of Building Fire Research’s strengths in research and standards, contributing to the enhancement of fire safety standards in power systems and safeguarding Macao’s electrical safety and sustainable development of energy.

This year marks the inaugural year of China’s 15th Five-Year Plan, with the concept of “energy powerhouse” being incorporated into a national five-year plan for the first time, while the development of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area (GBA) enters a new phase. Macao’s 3rd Five-Year Plan is also launching in tandem. As a core city of the GBA, Macao is actively integrating into the national development agenda and advancing its green and low-carbon transition. The stable and secure operation of power systems is critical to urban functioning, people's livelihoods, and the broader energy strategy. As large-scale renewable energy integration, energy storage facilities, and charging networks are rapidly deployed, fire safety in power systems faces new challenges. The cooperation between CEM and the CABR Institute of Building Fire Research not only provides robust technical support for CEM’s work in assisting the Macao SAR Government with urban renewal and improving electrical safety in residential buildings but also represents an important initiative in supporting the high-quality development of the GBA. Through this partnership, both parties will deepen collaborative innovation in the field of electrical fire safety, continuously driving Macao’s energy security and sustainable development forward.

澳門，2026年8月5日

為配合澳門城市發展及日益增長的用電需求，澳電積極投入資源優化輸配電網絡，致力提升供電系統的可靠度與穩定性。針對舊城區道路狹窄、交通繁忙的特點，澳電將於炮兵街的聖保祿變電站110千伏升級工程中，首度引入先進的「水平定向鑽工法」。工程以「地表交通零中斷」為核心理念，在提升本地輸電骨幹網絡及區內供電能力的同時，盡量減低工程對居民出行及商戶營運的影響。

作為澳電推動電網升級的重要項目之一，聖保祿變電站將由 66 千伏升級至 110 千伏。是次升級工程能減少電壓等級、提高電網效率，並擴充11千伏配網電纜饋線網絡，以實現更靈活的跨區電力調度，有效滿足核心區域及新發展區域日益增長的高質量用電需求。

由於工程沿線的鏡湖馬路為新橋區主要交通幹道，若採用傳統路面開挖方式，不但會影響交通，亦會干擾周邊醫院、學校及居民的日常運作與生活。經技術團隊審慎評估後，澳電決定引入在國際及鄰近各大城市已廣泛應用的「水平定向鑽工法」，鋪設一段由鴨涌河變電站連接至聖保祿變電站、全長約 900 米的 110 千伏高壓電纜。

該工程將由林茂海邊街起鑽，精準鑽入地下，經鏡湖馬路直達聖保祿變電站。施工期間，工程團隊將利用先進的導向鑽機進行地下鑽孔及擴孔，再將電纜一次性整體回拖入孔道內。此創新工法對維持區內交通順暢具備三大顯著優勢：\
1.直接免除了長達 900 米的封路及改道措施，讓鏡湖馬路及提督馬路等交通主幹道保持通行，並保障周邊醫院（如救護車進出）及學校的日常交通動線不受阻礙。\
2. 整項工程僅需於林茂海邊街（入土點）及炮兵巷（出土點）兩端設置工作坑，其餘相關工序均於地底完成，以「點狀」佔用取代傳統的「線狀」開挖，大幅釋放路面空間予車輛及行人。
3. 為減少日後因維修或擴容而重覆開挖道路，項目特別採用「一次施工、多組預留」的設計方案，預留備用管道及電纜。未來如出現故障或新增供電需求，可直接啟用備用設施，無需再次開挖路面，長遠免除對社區交通造成的二次負擔。

在確保交通順暢的同時，工程團隊嚴格把控施工安全與環境保護。團隊將採用先進的地下導向定位系統，精準控制鑽頭路徑，並持續監控、嚴格控制鑽孔與現有地下管線及建築物之間的距離，確保施工安全及避免對周邊建築物結構造成不良影響。現場亦會設置隔音布，夜間一律停止施工；同時透過閉環式泥漿循環鎖住泥沙，大幅減少粉塵排放。

由於必須滿足機器最小轉彎半徑，同時避免阻礙鄰近大廈及應急消防通道，現時位於林茂海邊街的開挖入土點，是唯一具備工程操作可行性的位置。受影響綠化帶內有三株樹木，包括兩株假菩提樹和一株芒果樹；為保留體積最大的假菩提樹，故只能選擇移植較小株的假菩提樹和芒果樹。澳電將妥善把假菩提樹移植至港珠澳人工島禁區的綠化帶，而芒果樹則移植到九澳聖母馬路的綠化帶。港珠澳人工島禁區綠化帶能為假菩提樹提供日照充足、空間寬闊的良好生長環境；而九澳聖母馬路綠化帶鄰近九澳山，芒果樹的花和果實可為野生動物提供食物來源，從而改善生態環境。

Macao, 5 August 2026

To align with Macao's urban development and growing electricity demand, Companhia de Electricidade de Macau - CEM, S.A. (CEM) is actively investing resources to optimize its transmission and distribution networks, dedicated to enhancing the reliability and stability of the power supply system. Addressing the narrow roads and heavy traffic characteristic of the old districts, CEM will introduce the advanced “Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD)” method for the first time in the 110kV upgrade project of the São Paulo Substation on Rua dos Artilheiros. Rooted in the core concept of “zero disruption to surface traffic,” the project aims to enhance the local transmission backbone network and the district’s power supply capacity while minimizing the impact on residents' travel and business operations.

As one of CEM's key initiatives to drive grid upgrades, the São Paulo Substation will be upgraded from 66kV to 110kV. This upgrade project will help eliminate intermediate voltage tiers, improve grid efficiency and expand the 11kV distribution cable feeder network. This enables more flexible cross-district power dispatching, effectively satisfying the growing demand for high-quality power supply in both core and new development areas.

Since Estrada do Repouso along the construction route is a major traffic artery in the San Kio district, traditional open-cut road excavation would not only impact traffic but also disrupt the daily operations and lives of nearby hospital, schools, and residents. Following a prudent evaluation by the technical team, CEM decided to introduce the HDD method, which has been widely utilized globally and in major neighboring cities, to lay a section of 110kV high-voltage cable, spanning approximately 900 meters, connecting the Canal dos Patos Substation to the São Paulo Substation.

The drilling will commence from Rua Marginal do Lam Mau, precisely boring deep underground and running through Estrada do Repousodirectly to the São Paulo Substation. During construction, the engineering team will utilize advanced drilling rigs to perform underground drilling and reaming, before pulling the entire cable back through the borehole in one go. This innovative construction method offers three significant advantages for maintaining smooth traffic flow in the district:
1. It eliminates the need for 900 meters of road closures and detours, keeping major traffic arteries like Estrada do Repouso and Avenida do Almirante Lacerda open. This ensures uninterrupted daily traffic flow for nearby hospitals (such as ambulance access) and schools.
2. The entire project only requires construction pits at both ends (the entry point at Rua Marginal do Lam Mauand the exit point at Travessa dos Artilheiros*)*, and all relevant processes are completed underground, replacing traditional "linear" excavation with a "point-based" footprint and freeing up substantial road space for vehicles and pedestrians.
3. The project specifically adopts a “single construction, multiple reservations” design concept with pre-installed spare ducts and cables to avoid repeated road excavations for future maintenance or capacity expansion. In the event of future faults or additional power demand, the backup facilities can be activated directly without re-excavating the roads, eliminating secondary traffic burdens on the community in the long run.

While ensuring smooth traffic flow, the project team is also strictly managing construction safety and environmental protection. The team will employ an advanced underground guidance and positioning system to precisely control the drill head's path, and will continuously monitor and strictly control the distance between the borehole and existing underground utilities and buildings, so as to ensure construction safety and prevent any adverse impact on the structures of adjacent buildings. Acoustic curtains will be installed on-site, and all construction work will cease at night. Additionally, a closed-loop mud circulation system will be utilized to trap silt and sand, significantly reducing dust emissions.

Due to the machinery's minimum turning radius requirements and the need to keep adjacent buildings and emergency access routes clear, the current excavation entry point at Rua Marginal do Lam Mau is the only location feasible for construction operations. There are three trees in the affected green belt, including two Mock Bodhi trees and one mango tree. To preserve the largest Mock Bodhi tree on-site, only the smaller Mock Bodhi tree and the mango tree will be transplanted.

CEM will be carefully relocate the Mock Bodhi tree to the green belts within the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge artificial island’s restricted area and the mango tree to the green belt along Estrada de Nossa Senhora de Ká Hó. The green belt on the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge artificial island’s restricted area provides a spacious and optimal growing environment with ample sunlight for the Mock Bodhi tree, while the green belt on Estrada de Nossa Senhora de Ká Hó is close to the Ká Hó mountain, where the flowers and fruit of the mango tree can provide food sources for wild animals and enhance the ecological environment.

CEM has always attached great importance to the community environment and public opinions and is actively coordinating with relevant government departments on preliminary publicity, which includes engaging with environmental greening and hygiene focus group of Consultative Committee on Municipal Affairs under the Municipal Affairs Bureau and the Central District Community Service Consultative Council. Earlier, CEM visited the General Union of Neighbor Association of Macao (UGAMM), the Patane Residents’ Mutual Aid Association and the Affiliated Kindergarten of Lou Hau High School to gather opinions on the project. During construction, CEM will display promotional banners on site hoardings to keep local residents informed about the project. Moving forward, CEM will continue to maintain close communication with other community organizations and release further updates to the public in a timely manner, aiming to secure community understanding and cooperation.

Todo o projecto requer apenas valas de construção em ambas as extremidades (o ponto de entrada na Rua Marginal do Lam Mau e o ponto de saída na Travessa dos Artilheiros), e todos os processos relevantes são concluídos no subsolo, substituindo a escavação “linear” tradicional por uma pegada “pontual” e libertando espaço considerável nas vias para veículos e peões.
3. O projecto adopta especificamente um conceito de projecto de "construção única, múltiplas reservas" com condutas e cabos sobressalentes pré-instalados para evitar escavações repetidas nas vias para manutenção futura ou expansão da capacidade. Em caso de falhas futuras ou de procura adicional de energia, as instalações de reserva podem ser activadas directamente sem necessidade de reescavar as vias, eliminando, a longo prazo, os encargos secundários de tráfego para a comunidade.

Ao mesmo tempo que garante o fluxo contínuo de tráfego, a equipa de projecto está também a gerir rigorosamente a segurança da construção e a protecção ambiental. A equipa utilizará um sistema avançado de orientação e posicionamento subterrâneo para controlar com precisão a trajectória da cabeça de perfuração e monitorizará continuamente e controlará rigorosamente a distância entre o furo e as instalações subterrâneas e edifícios existentes, de forma a garantir a segurança da construção e evitar qualquer impacto adverso nas estruturas dos edifícios adjacentes. Serão instaladas cortinas acústicas no local e todos os trabalhos de construção serão interrompidos à noite. Além disso, será utilizado um sistema de circulação de lamas em circuito fechado para reter o lodo e a areia, reduzindo significativamente a emissão de poeiras.

Devido aos requisitos de raio de viragem mínimo das máquinas e à necessidade de manter desobstruídos os edifícios adjacentes e as vias de acesso de emergência, o actual ponto de entrada da escavação na Rua Marginal do Lam Mau é o único local viável para as operações de construção. Existem três árvores na zona verde afectada, incluindo duas árvores Mock Bodhi e uma mangueira. Para preservar a maior Mock Bodhi no local, apenas a Mock Bodhi mais pequena e a mangueira serão transplantadas.

A CEM irá mudar cuidadosamente a árvore Mock Bodhi para o cinturão verde dentro da área restrita da ilha artificial da Ponte Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau e a mangueira para o cinturão verde ao longo da Estrada de Nossa Senhora de Ká Hó. O cinturão verde na área restrita da ilha artificial da Ponte Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau oferece um ambiente de crescimento amplo e ideal, com bastante luz solar para a árvore Mock Bodhi, enquanto o cinturão verde na Estrada de Nossa Senhora de Ká Hó fica próximo da montanha de Ká Hó, onde as flores e os frutos da mangueira podem servir de alimento para animais selvagens e melhorar o ambiente ecológico.

A CEM sempre atribuiu grande importância ao ambiente comunitário e à opinião pública, coordenando activamente com os departamentos governamentais relevantes a divulgação preliminar, o que inclui o envolvimento com o grupo de trabalho sobre arborização e higiene ambiental do Comité Consultivo de Assuntos Municipais, ligado ao Instituto de Assuntos Municipais, e com o Conselho Consultivo de Serviços Comunitários da Zona Central. Anteriormente, a CEM visitou a União Geral das Associações dos Moradores de Macau (UGAMM), a Associação de Ajuda Mútua dos Moradores do Patane e o Jardim de Infância Afiliado à Escola Secundária Lou Hau para recolher opiniões sobre o projecto. Durante a construção, a CEM irá exibir faixas promocionais em tapumes no local para manter os residentes locais informados sobre o projecto. No futuro, a CEM continuará a manter uma comunicação estreita com outras organizações comunitárias e divulgará as actualizações ao público em tempo útil, visando garantir a compreensão e a cooperação da comunidade.

Featuring three voltage levels of 330kV, 110kV, and 10kV, the substation became one of the province's first smart inspection pilot projects in 2021, equipped with over a hundred cameras and robots to achieve comprehensive smart monitoring. The 10kV Taohai Switching Station, commissioned in August 2025, serves as a core hub of the Anning power grid and a key unit of the self-healing demonstration zone. It is equipped with four types of standby automatic power input protection methods to ensure stable power supply.

Established in October 2023, the Command Center of State Grid Lanzhou Company serves as the "command hub" for production safety and emergency power supply assurance. It shoulders the role of the command hub for production safety, service assurance, and emergency response, and is responsible for ten core businesses—including safety control, production control, emergency control, fire safety control, comprehensive duty management, operational display, power supply service command, emergency power guarantee, professional support, and system operation—thereby achieving agile online and offline control.

Localizada no Distrito de Anning, a uma altitude de 1.600 metros e com uma área de 50 mu (aproximadamente 20 hectares), a Subestação de Lanzhou, de 330kV, em Taoshucun, iniciou as suas operações em 1997 e, posteriormente, foi alvo de actualizações de tensão e modernização de equipamentos. Com três níveis de tensão (330kV, 110kV e 10kV), a subestação tornou-se um dos primeiros projectos-piloto de inspecção inteligente da província em 2021, equipada com mais de cem câmaras e robôs para realizar uma monitorização inteligente abrangente. A Estação de Comutação de 10kV em Taohai, comissionada em Agosto de 2025, serve como um hub essencial da rede eléctrica de Anning e uma unidade-chave da zona de demonstração de autosustentabilidade. Está equipada com quatro tipos de métodos automáticos de protecção de entrada de energia em modo de ‘standby’ para garantir um fornecimento de energia estável.

Instituído em Outubro de 2023, o Centro de Comando da State Grid Lanzhou Company serve como "hub de comando" para a segurança da produção e garantia do fornecimento de energia em situações de emergência. Assume o papel de hub de comando para a segurança da produção, garantia de serviços e resposta a emergências, sendo responsável por dez áreas principais: controlo de segurança, controlo de produção, controlo de emergências, controlo de segurança contra incêndios, gestão abrangente de tarefas, execução operacional, comando de serviços de fornecimento de energia, garantia de energia em situações de emergência, suporte técnico e operação do sistema, conseguindo assim um controlo ágil online e offline.

Através desta visita, os membros da CLC irão experienciar em primeira mão os principais avanços do país em novas energias e redes inteligentes, proporcionando informações e referências valiosas para Macau aprofundar ainda mais a construção de cidades inteligentes, optimizar os serviços de fornecimento de energia e promover a educação em ciências verdes no futuro.
